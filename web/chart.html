How each metric is computed

Every figure comes from XBRL tags filed with the SEC — nothing is estimated or modelled. Ratios are computed period by period from the two underlying series, never by mixing periods.

What the data covers, and what it cannot

Single source: XBRL filings with the SEC — the ~10,400 companies listed in the United States, in both the us-gaap and ifrs-full taxonomies. Foreign companies listed there are included (ASML in EUR, Novo Nordisk in DKK, TSMC in TWD). Figures are shown in the currency they were reported in. A company not listed in the US has no SEC filings at all: Constellation Software or Hermès cannot be found here.

Quarterly and TTM: cash-flow statements are filed cumulatively from the start of the fiscal year. Discrete quarters are rebuilt by differencing consecutive cumulative filings, and the four quarters of a year are checked against the reported annual total.

Comparing a quarter to a year is misleading for seasonal businesses. Use TTM when you want a figure comparable to the annual one.

Data source: SEC EDGAR (XBRL).
