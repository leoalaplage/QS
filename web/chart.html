The SEC sends no CORS headers, so a static site cannot call it directly. The worker/ folder in the repository holds the relay to deploy (free, ~50 lines). The URL is stored in this browser only.

What the data covers

Single source: XBRL filings with the SEC. That covers the ~10,400 companies listed in the United States, in both the us-gaap and ifrs-full taxonomies — so foreign companies listed there are included (ASML in EUR, Novo Nordisk in DKK, TSMC in TWD, SAP in EUR). Figures are shown in the currency they were reported in.

Out of reach: a company that is not listed in the United States has no SEC filings at all — Constellation Software (Toronto) or Hermès (Paris) cannot be found here, whatever you search for.

Quarterly and TTM: both need detailed quarterly reports (10-Q). Foreign filers often publish an annual report only (20-F), in which case annual is the only period available — and the site says so instead of drawing an empty chart. Since Q4 is never filed in a 10-Q, it is reconstructed by difference with the fiscal year, and that calculation is checked against the years where all four quarters already exist.

Data source: SEC EDGAR (XBRL) — extraction derived from qs_chart.py.
