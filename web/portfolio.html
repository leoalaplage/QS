Portfolio report

Paste your holdings and get a one-page A4 PNG: allocation, sectors, geography, market-cap breakdown, weighted fundamentals and, if you give a cost basis, performance. Fundamentals come from XBRL filings with the SEC.

Positions quoted in another currency are converted at nothing — they are reported in their own currency and flagged, rather than mixed at a rate the site does not have.

How the weighted figures are built

A portfolio has no gross margin — its holdings do. Every aggregate is a weighted average by position size, and the weights are renormalised over the lines that actually report the figure. A holding with no published gross margin — Visa, which declares no cost of revenue — drops out of that average instead of dragging it down, and the coverage is printed whenever it is below 100 % of the book.

Valuation ratios are weighted by value, never by line count: a 1 % position must not weigh as much as a 30 % one in the portfolio P/E.

Sectors come from the built-in table, falling back to the SEC’s own industry description. Geography is the country of the business address on file with the SEC — the place the company reports from, not where it earns its revenue.

Data source: SEC EDGAR (XBRL) and market quotes.
